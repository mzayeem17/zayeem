AI-assisted campaign trafficking and QA

At DataBeat, I built AI-assisted workflows for campaign trafficking, QA, and reporting. Internal time tracking found that employees using deployed workflows spent about 30% less time on tasks.

Role
Team Lead, AdOps & Yield
Role period

Tools

What I built

I built custom skills for repeatable trafficking, QA, and reporting tasks alongside the team’s SOPs and QA processes. The aim was to reduce manual work on recurring checks and reporting.

What an assisted check can look like

What we measured

Internal time tracking showed approximately 30% lower average task completion time among employees using deployed workflows. This is an aggregate for the workflows in use. The tracking period, sample size, and task-level split are unavailable for this write-up. Yield agents are still in development.
