Growing publisher revenue across two portfolios

At Z1 Tech, I led publisher yield work that recorded 20%+ revenue growth across enterprise publishers and 30%+ uplift across mid-tier partners.

Role
Associate Manager, AdOps & Yield
Role period
Feb 2025 – May 2026
Team
Four associates, with product and demand partners

The problem

Across the two portfolios, we had to find where revenue was constrained. Floor pricing and demand priority were part of the picture, alongside fill, auction behavior, inventory forecasts, and delivery problems.

What I led

I managed four associates working on yield and troubleshooting. I worked with the product and demand teams on auction logic, floor strategy, and demand-stack priorities. The work also included investigating tag and ad-verification issues that affected delivery.

How the work fit together

Results and measurement

The work recorded 20%+ revenue growth across enterprise publishers and 30%+ uplift across mid-tier partners. These are reported portfolio outcomes from the role. The exact comparison window and baseline figures are unavailable for this write-up, so the contribution of each individual change is open.
