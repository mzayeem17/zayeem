What to check before changing a publisher price floor

A floor change can raise eCPM while reducing revenue. Before deciding whether to roll one out, compare what happened per ad request across similar inventory.

Start with the segment

Pick a placement, device group, and demand mix you can compare over time. If the composition changes during the test, split the analysis before attributing movement to the new floor.

A worked example

Suppose a placement gets one million ad requests. At 75% fill and $2.00 eCPM, it earns $1,500. At 60% fill and $2.40 eCPM, the same request volume earns $1,440. In this illustrative example, eCPM rises 20% while revenue falls 4%.

What would change the decision?

Check whether the loss is concentrated in specific placements or devices. Compare revenue per thousand ad requests and, when page views are available, page RPM. Set an acceptable fill change before the test and use a comparable group or period to judge the tradeoff. If the result varies by segment, test the floor where the numbers support it before rolling it out further.
