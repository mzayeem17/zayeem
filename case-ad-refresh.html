Ad refresh on display

Ad refresh was one of several changes I worked on as a senior analyst and account manager at DataBeat. This note covers what to measure when testing refresh on existing display inventory.

The decision

Decide which placements are eligible for refresh and how to compare the outcome with equivalent inventory. Extra impressions are useful if they improve page-level yield while maintaining viewability and the reader experience.

What to measure

  • Page RPM. Revenue per thousand page views.
  • Revenue. Compare like-for-like placements and periods, accounting for changes in traffic and other monetization work.
  • Viewability. Track the share of measurable impressions that were viewable. Check the proposed refresh behavior against demand requirements.

What we observed

Publisher RPM and fill improved across the broader yield work, which included lazy loading and pricing-rule changes. Isolating refresh would require a comparable group without refresh and a defined measurement window.
